# Replay result

A Replay's result file (ARCHITECTURE.md sections 3.12 and 6) holds the plan hash, the check hash and its ledger entry, the start point and `prefix_edits`, and one entry per sample: whether it was a tiebreak sample, its status (`pass`, `fail` or `unassessed`), whether its pass is discounted, its attempts with their status, the result and taint of each expectation, and the intent judgment with its reason. It also says whether tiebreak samples ran, the baseline counts, whether the moment meets the check and whether it improved, the verdict (`fails`, `meets-check` or `incomplete`), the discount and how it was confirmed, and the fidelity grade. When the plan let the box reach the internet, `network` gives the level, `used_live_internet`, and the hosts reached live and refused across samples; `used_live_internet: true` means the replay was not a frozen world. A sample's `answers.live` counts the shell lines that reached a live host, and `answers.live_calls` names each one with its hosts. Every connection the box tried is in the sample trace as a `network.connection` meta event on the `sandbox` stream, with host, port, method (null for TLS, whose method is encrypted), whether it was let through, why not, and the shell lines running at the time.
